## The short version

Your dog lives on your phone, and so does your data. Dog Years has no account and no
server. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information, and there are no ads
or analytics.

## What the app uses, and where it stays

| Data | Why | Where it stays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Step count (Apple Health / Health Connect), if you allow it | So your dog knows when you have walked or sat for a long time | Read on your phone; 10-minute totals are stored on your phone only |
| Screen time on apps you choose, if you allow it | So your dog reacts to long social media sessions | On your phone. App names and package names are never stored by the app on iOS (Apple only gives the app an opaque token) and never leave your phone on Android |
| Walks you log, water glasses, pets, mood check-ins and the notes you type | Your dog's day and your progress | On your phone only |
| Your dog's name, breed, wake time, bedtime and settings | To run the app | On your phone only |
| Notifications | Up to 3 gentle reminders a day from your dog | Scheduled on your phone; no push server is used |

The app does not use location, contacts, photos, the microphone or the camera.

## Health data

Health data read from Apple Health or Health Connect is used only to show your dog's
reactions and your progress. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared
with third parties and never sent off your phone. You can turn off access at any time in
the Health app (iOS) or Health Connect (Android).

## Purchases

[WHEN M9 SHIPS] Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to check
whether you have Premium; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase
history, not your health, mood or screen-time data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

## Your choices and rights

- Every permission is optional. The app works with manual walk logging if you allow nothing.
- **Delete everything:** Settings → Your data → Delete all data removes your dog and every
  record from your phone. Deleting the app does the same.
- Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to access,
  correct or delete on request; you can still contact us at [PRIVACY EMAIL].
